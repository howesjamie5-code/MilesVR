MilesVR 0.1.65 - TEST BUILD for Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales
=================================================================

What changed since 0.1.64: WALL STEPS TURNED OFF
--------------------------------------------------
0.1.64's stepping legs on walls looked silly and buggy, so they are off:
on a wall Miles's feet stand under his hips as in 0.1.63. The feet still
step after you on the ground in your room, and the perch stays.

What changed since 0.1.63: WALKING LEGS ON WALLS
--------------------------------------------------
Walking a wall (the left stick while you stand on it), Miles's feet now
walk too: each foot stays where it stands on the wall, then steps ahead
in the way you go, left and right in turn, quicker the faster you walk -
no more sliding. The same steps show anywhere the game moves him while
his legs are planted.

What to try:
1. Stand on a wall, walk with the stick slowly and quickly: do the legs
   walk? Too fast, too slow, too big steps?

What changed since 0.1.62: WALKING LEGS, STEPPING FEET AND A PERCH
--------------------------------------------------
- Moving with the left stick on the ground plays the game's walk and run
  legs again (0.1.62 kept them planted).
- Standing still, each foot stays put. When you move about your room and
  your body gets about 18 cm from a foot, it takes a quick step (lifts,
  moves, lands), one foot at a time, instead of sliding.
- Perched (the game holds Miles in a low crouch for half a second or
  more: a lamp post, a ledge, a rooftop edge): a Spider-Man perch - feet
  wider apart, knees bent out to the sides. How low he crouches follows
  your real head: crouch down and he sinks into the perch, stand up and he
  rises out of it.
- Charging a Charge Jump, jumping and the air keep the game's legs.

What to try:
1. Walk with the stick: walking legs? Stand still and step about in your
   room: do the feet step?
2. Land on a lamp post or ledge and crouch down in real life: the perch?

What changed since 0.1.61: NO CROUCHING LEGS ON THE GROUND
--------------------------------------------------
On the ground Miles's legs no longer fold into the game's crouches (the
squat after landing, crouching on a perch, sneaking): he stands on
planted legs, feet flat under the hips. The game's own leg animation
still plays while you charge a Charge Jump (hold A), when you press jump
(A), and whenever you are off the ground (swinging, falling).

What to try:
1. Land from a swing, stand on a lamp post or ledge: legs standing, no
   squat?
2. Hold A on the ground: the charge crouch still shows? Jump: the jump's
   legs?

What changed since 0.1.60: BIG GROUND EXPLOSIONS FOR THE SMASH AND THE MEGA
--------------------------------------------------
Venom Smash (punch down): a white-hot flash on the ground where you land,
three orange shockwave rings racing out across the ground to the blast's
5 m edge, and electric cracks splitting along the ground.
Mega Venom Blast: the same out to its 10 m edge, plus a tall pillar of
orange light shooting up from where you stand with bolts arcing up out of
it, and a bigger flash. It lasts about a second.
(The Venom Jump gets the smash's ground effect too.)

What to try:
1. Venom smash, then a Mega Venom Blast: do they look like big
   explosions now? Too bright to see through?

What changed since 0.1.59: A BIGGER, BRIGHTER VENOM PUNCH
--------------------------------------------------
The venom punch's burst is now an explosion: a bigger white-hot flash
that holds a quarter of a second, an orange shockwave ring blasting out
to about 1.5 m, twice the bolts and longer ones, and an afterglow. The
smash and the Mega Venom Blast get the same, their flash kept from
whiting out your view.

What to try:
1. Venom punch an enemy: does it feel forceful now? Too much?

It was far too loud and stopped abruptly. Now:
- about half as loud, and less distorted;
- 2.5 seconds long, its rumble fading out smoothly over the last second
  instead of stopping;
- nothing cuts it short: the punch sounds of the enemies it hits wait
  until it has finished.

What to try:

What to try:

What changed since 0.1.48: WEB BALLS OFF THE SWING BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------
The grip's tap web ball (0.1.42-0.1.48) interfered with swinging in a
fight and with camouflage: a quick web counted as a tap and fired a web
ball. So the web button is your webs and swinging alone again.
With your settings (Web button: Grip, Venom button: Trigger):
- Grip: webs and swinging only, on both hands.
- Trigger, no web: a web ball, the moment you pull it (as originally).
- Trigger, web attached: reel in.
- Venom full, right trigger: TAP for a web ball, HOLD to charge.
The other way round (Web button: Trigger, Venom button: Grip) swaps the
grip and the trigger. Your other hand works as normal.

What changed since 0.1.47: WEB BALLS ONLY FROM A TAP OF THE WEB BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------
Web balls (and a gadget picked on the wheel) now come only from a short
tap of the web button, on both hands. The other button (the venom
button) no longer shoots web balls: it reels in with a web attached, and
on the venom hand it charges the venom once a bar is full.
With your settings (Web button: Grip, Venom button: Trigger):
- Grip: tap = web ball, hold = web-swing.
- Trigger: reel in with a web attached; with venom full, hold the right
  trigger to charge. With no web and no venom it does nothing.
The other way round (Web button: Trigger, Venom button: Grip) swaps them.

What to try:
1. Tap a grip at an enemy: a web ball. Pull a trigger with no web:
   nothing.
2. Pick a gadget on the wheel (hold X): a tap of the grip fires it.

What changed since 0.1.46: WEB BUTTON AND VENOM BUTTON ARE NEVER THE SAME
--------------------------------------------------
Web button and Venom button are now always opposite buttons. Change one
(in the launcher or the game's MILES VR settings) and the other switches
with it. Only two setups exist:
- Web button GRIP, Venom button TRIGGER: the grip always webs (tap: web
  ball, hold: swing) on BOTH hands; the trigger is normal until venom is
  full, then holding the venom hand's trigger charges the venom.
- Web button TRIGGER, Venom button GRIP: the other way round - the
  trigger always webs (tap: web ball, hold: swing) on both hands; the
  venom hand's grip charges the venom once a bar is full.
The launcher's VENOM POWERS card says which setup is yours ("Web And
Venom Buttons"), and the WEBS card lists the web button's tap and hold.
Saved settings with both on the same button (the old default was both
on Grip) are fixed as they load: the web button stays, the venom goes to
the other one.

What changed since 0.1.32: VENOM JUMP AND VENOM DASH

venom hand's grip until the fist glows (the controller buzzes). Each uses

  Every enemy within 5 m is hit with venom and thrown up into the air, and

0.1.5: point and click in the game's menus:
NEW: point and click in the game's menus. On any menu shown on the screen in
the headset (pause, map, suits, skills, settings, the title screen), point a
hand at the screen: the game's mouse cursor goes where you point. That hand's
TRIGGER clicks (hold it to drag), and its STICK up or down scrolls. Point with

0.1.4 (still in this build): the crash after a while on walls is fixed:
0.1.3 played in VR, swinging included (73 webs and 48 zips in the first
session), then crashed after about nine minutes. The cause: on a wall, Spidy
gives the game's "interaction offers" (vents, doors) a crawl mask, and Miles
Morales keeps that mask 8 bytes further on - where Remastered had the mask,
Miles Morales has a pointer. Spidy overwrote it, and the game crashed reading
through it the next time it looked for something to interact with. Fixed, and
every place Spidy writes into the game's memory has now been checked against
Miles Morales.

Before that, 0.1.3 fixed the missing eye images of 0.1.2:
0.1.2 no longer crashed: the menus showed in the headset and VR took over the
camera, then stopped with "No native eye images reached OpenXR within 3
seconds" (code 1). Miles Morales keeps the eye's render job 16 bytes further
into the game's render context than Remastered did, so Spidy never saw an eye
image. Fixed, with three more fields of the same kind:
- the render context's anti-aliasing switch (+0x424, was +0x40c): Spidy was
  writing to the wrong field;
- the HUD panel's texture flag (+0xd0, was +0xc0);
- the web ropes' bookkeeping after the 16 rope slots (0x100 further on), which
  the webs you see while swinging use.

Before that, 0.1.2 fixed the crash when the headset began tracking:
0.1.0 and 0.1.1 crashed the moment the headset started tracking, found by
switching Spidy's VR hooks off in halves with the headset on: the hook on the
game's view update. Miles Morales's version of that function takes a second
argument (a flag that makes it resize the view's render buffers) that Spider-Man
Remastered's did not, and the hook passed only the first, so the game resized
buffers at random and the GPU driver crashed. The hook now passes both. Every
other hooked function was checked the same way; only the (still disabled)
SPIDY VR settings tab has the same kind of change.

0.1.1 also fixed START VR so it starts the game itself again: close the game
before you press START VR and let the launcher start it.
