MilesVR 0.1.14 - TEST BUILD for Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales
=================================================================

What changed since 0.1.13: LEFT HAND VENOM STARTS VR
--------------------------------------------------
0.1.13 with Venom punch hand set to Left hand stopped with "Game XR start:
1001": the VR module refused the new setting's switch as out of range.
Fixed. Everything else is as in 0.1.13 below.

What changed since 0.1.12: VENOM PUNCH HAND IN THE LAUNCHER
--------------------------------------------------
The launcher's OPTIONS have a new setting, "Venom punch hand":
- Right hand (the default): as in 0.1.12. With the venom meter full the right
  hand's webs switch off; hold the RIGHT GRIP to charge, punch with the right
  fist.
- Left hand: the same with the left. With the meter full the LEFT hand's webs
  switch off; hold the LEFT GRIP to charge, punch the marked enemy with the
  left fist. The right hand keeps swinging.
The choice is saved with your other options, and takes effect at the next
START VR.

What to try:
1. In the launcher, set Venom punch hand to Left hand, then START VR.
2. Fill the meter: do the LEFT webs stop? Hold the left grip, punch the
   marked enemy with your left fist.
3. Set it back to Right hand and check the right still works.

What changed since 0.1.11: THE VENOM PUNCH AFTER A RESTART
--------------------------------------------------
Your 0.1.10 test: the venom punch worked and the meter emptied; then you
restarted the challenge, the meter was full again, and the right grip only
shot webs. A restart makes the game build Miles's melee data anew, and the
mod kept reading the old copy (still empty from your punch). It now checks
every frame that it reads the game's current one, and finds the new one
within a second.

Same controls as 0.1.10, and the session report records the venom meter
(0.1.11).

What to try:
1. Fill the meter, hold the right grip until the rumble, punch toward the
   marked enemy.
2. Restart the challenge (or keep fighting) until the meter is full again,
   and do it again - a few times.
3. Tell Claude how it went, and keep the reports folder.

What changed since 0.1.10: THE VENOM METER IN THE SESSION REPORT
--------------------------------------------------
Same controls as 0.1.10 (venom punch on the right grip). Your 0.1.10 test:
one venom punch landed (84 damage, at 23 s), then the right grip shot webs.
The mod only switches the right webs off while the meter is completely
FULL, and after that punch only five more of your blows landed in the next
2.5 minutes, so the meter most likely never filled again. This build writes
the meter, the grip and every charge and venom punch into the session report,
so the next test shows exactly what happened.

What to try:
1. Fill the meter, hold the right grip until the rumble, punch toward the
   marked enemy.
2. Then keep fighting until the meter on screen is FULL again, and try again.
3. Tell Claude: after the venom punch, did the meter on screen empty? And
   when the grip shot webs, did the meter on screen look full?

What changed since 0.1.9: THE VENOM PUNCH IS ON THE RIGHT GRIP
--------------------------------------------------
Your idea: the venom punch moves from the right stick to the RIGHT GRIP.
(In 0.1.9, holding the right stick in also switched on the pad layer - the
both-sticks mode - which cancelled the charge.)
- When the venom meter is FULL, the right controller's webs switch OFF (no
  web, no web ball, no aim marker on that hand) and the right hand rumbles.
  The left hand swings as usual. A web the right hand is holding when the
  meter fills stays until you let go, so you don't drop out of a swing.
- HOLD the RIGHT GRIP: the fist crackles with gold venom; a strong rumble
  says it is charged (about a third of a second).
- PUNCH toward the MARKED enemy with your right fist: within 2 m it lands on
  him at once; farther, the web strike flies you to him and lands it.
- The meter empties, and the right webs come back.
- The right stick's click is LB + RB again (doors and grates), but never in
  a fight, so it throws nothing at the enemies.

What to try:
1. Fill the meter. Do the right webs stop, with a rumble?
2. Hold the right grip until the rumble. Does the fist glow?
3. Punch toward the marked enemy, up close and from across the room. Does he
   fly off stunned, does the meter empty, and do the right webs come back?

What changed since 0.1.8: THE VENOM PUNCH LANDS, NO MORE THROWS
--------------------------------------------------
Your 0.1.8 test: the venom charged, but the punch never landed, and the right
stick's click sometimes threw objects at the enemies. Both are fixed:
- The venom punch no longer needs your fist to touch the enemy (in Miles
  Morales the mod's fists almost never register a touch: 3 in four sessions,
  against 33 web strikes). Charged, throw a right punch toward the MARKED
  enemy (the one with the target ring): within 2 m it lands on him at once;
  farther, the web strike flies you to him and lands it on arrival.
- In a fight (an enemy within reach now or in the last 3 seconds) the right
  stick's click never sends LB + RB, so no more throwing objects. Outside a
  fight it still pulls doors and grates.

What to try:
1. Fill the venom meter, hold the right stick's click until the rumble.
2. Look at an enemy (the ring marks him) and punch toward him with your right
   fist, up close and from across the room.
3. Does he fly off stunned, and does the meter on screen empty?
4. Click the right stick in a fight with the meter not full: nothing should
   be thrown.

What changed since 0.1.7: THE VENOM PUNCH
--------------------------------------------------
Miles's venom punch, as you designed it:
- When the venom meter is FULL, the right hand rumbles once (the launcher's
  window says "Venom full").
- HOLD the RIGHT STICK'S CLICK: your right fist crackles with gold venom,
  growing for about a third of a second; a strong rumble says it is charged.
- PUNCH with your right fist: at an enemy in reach, or swing at the marked
  enemy (the target ring) across the room and the web strike flies you in.
  That blow is the venom punch: a flash of venom bolts where it lands, the
  game's own venom damage and stun, and he is sent flying. The meter empties.
- You can let go of the stick once it is charged: the charge stays for 1.5 s.
- While the meter is full, the right stick's click does NOT do LB + RB (doors,
  grates). Below full it does, as before. (Clicking both sticks is still the
  pad layer, and charges nothing.)

The meter was found from your 0.1.7 recording (thank you): it filled 6.5 a
hit and the two heals spent it. The recorder is gone from this build.

What to try (about 5 minutes, the training sim is ideal):
1. Fight until the venom meter is full. Do you feel the rumble?
2. Hold the right stick's click. Does the fist glow and crackle, then rumble?
3. Punch an enemy with the right fist. Does he fly, stunned, and does the
   game's venom meter on screen empty?
4. Try it from across the room too (swing at the marked enemy).
5. Below full, does the right stick's click still pull a door or grate?
Tell Claude what happened, and keep the reports folder.

0.1.5: point and click in the game's menus:
NEW: point and click in the game's menus. On any menu shown on the screen in
the headset (pause, map, suits, skills, settings, the title screen), point a
hand at the screen: the game's mouse cursor goes where you point. That hand's
TRIGGER clicks (hold it to drag), and its STICK up or down scrolls. Point with
either hand; pulling the other hand's trigger while it points takes over.
Everything else still works as an Xbox controller (A, B, X, Y, the other stick).
Not while you play the game on the flat screen (SWITCH TO GAME SCREEN), where
the mouse would turn the camera - pause there and it works again.

0.1.4 (still in this build): the crash after a while on walls is fixed:
0.1.3 played in VR, swinging included (73 webs and 48 zips in the first
session), then crashed after about nine minutes. The cause: on a wall, Spidy
gives the game's "interaction offers" (vents, doors) a crawl mask, and Miles
Morales keeps that mask 8 bytes further on - where Remastered had the mask,
Miles Morales has a pointer. Spidy overwrote it, and the game crashed reading
through it the next time it looked for something to interact with. Fixed, and
every place Spidy writes into the game's memory has now been checked against
Miles Morales.

Before that, 0.1.3 fixed the missing eye images of 0.1.2:
0.1.2 no longer crashed: the menus showed in the headset and VR took over the
camera, then stopped with "No native eye images reached OpenXR within 3
seconds" (code 1). Miles Morales keeps the eye's render job 16 bytes further
into the game's render context than Remastered did, so Spidy never saw an eye
image. Fixed, with three more fields of the same kind:
- the render context's anti-aliasing switch (+0x424, was +0x40c): Spidy was
  writing to the wrong field;
- the HUD panel's texture flag (+0xd0, was +0xc0);
- the web ropes' bookkeeping after the 16 rope slots (0x100 further on), which
  the webs you see while swinging use.

Before that, 0.1.2 fixed the crash when the headset began tracking:
0.1.0 and 0.1.1 crashed the moment the headset started tracking, found by
switching Spidy's VR hooks off in halves with the headset on: the hook on the
game's view update. Miles Morales's version of that function takes a second
argument (a flag that makes it resize the view's render buffers) that Spider-Man
Remastered's did not, and the hook passed only the first, so the game resized
buffers at random and the GPU driver crashed. The hook now passes both. Every
other hooked function was checked the same way; only the (still disabled)
SPIDY VR settings tab has the same kind of change.

0.1.1 also fixed START VR so it starts the game itself again: close the game
before you press START VR and let the launcher start it.

An unofficial port of Spidy 0.3.8 by Ilya Mezerowsky (GPL-3.0,
https://github.com/IlyaMez/SpidyVR) from Spider-Man Remastered to Miles Morales.
Not made or endorsed by Ilya, Insomniac, Sony or Marvel. Please do not report
problems with this build to Spidy's Discord: they are this port's, not Spidy's.

This build has NOT been run in a headset yet. It was ported offline: every game
address Spidy uses was translated from Spider-Man.exe to MilesMorales.exe and
every one of its 126 code checks passes against your MilesMorales.exe
(4.630.0.0, Steam build 23986141). If a check failed in the game, VR would stop
with a code instead of crashing.

What to test (in this order)
----------------------------
1. Start "MilesVR Launcher.exe". The game line should name Marvel's Spider-Man:
   Miles Morales and show it as supported. Connect Virtual Desktop, CLOSE THE
   GAME, press START VR: the launcher starts the game (a 1920 x 1080 window).

4. SWINGING: grip to fire a web from that hand, swing on it, release; trigger

5. Look down: Miles's body and arms should follow your controllers.

Known gaps in 0.1
-----------------
- No SPIDY VR tab in the game's Settings: Miles Morales lays out its settings
  menu differently. Set options in the launcher instead (the session says
  "No SPIDY VR tab ... (9300)" - that is expected).
- Combat, grabbing, gadgets, prompts and slow motion are ported but untested;
  the venom punch is new in 0.1.8; Miles's other venom powers are not here yet.
- The launcher's update check is off (Spidy's own releases are for Remastered).
