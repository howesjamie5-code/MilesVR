# Wall running

With HAND WALL CLIMBING enabled, the left stick switches from the close
climbing view to the running view while attached to an outdoor wall.
Releasing it restores the close view. Moving the stick on an ordinary
floor does not request an automatic wall mount. Head yaw steers wall
running; looking down cannot reverse forward travel.

Momentum from swinging can also carry the player along a wall without
stick input. The running body's orientation follows surface travel, with
blends between horizontal, diagonal and vertical poses. The tracked head
and hands stay under player control. Visible footfalls use long alternating
steps; high travel speed does not speed the leg cycle beyond its normal
running cadence. A confirmed wall grip restores crawling. Vents use their
own crawling mode and do not enter wall running.

The joystick pose uses a straight upper body leaning 15 degrees and a
camera distance of 110 cm. Horizontal momentum running uses 30 degrees
camera tilt, 90 cm clearance, 50 degrees body tilt, 30 degrees forward lean,
and normal step length/pace. These fixed values replace the experimental
tuning sliders; older numeric settings are ignored. WALL RUN CAMERA TILT
remains an on/off switch, enabled by default, respecting saved choices.
Momentum values blend toward the vertical pose as travel turns upward.

Validation covers surface travel, cadence, spine alignment, head placement,
camera clearance, ground/vent exclusions and native command compatibility.
The player tested the animation on Quest 3 through Virtual Desktop.
